# SettleZero --- User Journey 5 v0.1

## Admin / Member Lifecycle

This document defines the lifecycle of members, administrators, devices,
group ownership, archiving, and related administrative actions in
SettleZero.

The central principle is:

> **Membership identity and device identity are separate.**

A member can exist without a connected device, and a device can be
replaced without changing the member's historical identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Member Management Screen

An Admin-only management screen provides a clear view of group
membership and roles.

Example:

> **Members**
>
> **Admins**
>
> 👑 Sayantan --- Super Admin
>
> 🛡️ Priya --- Admin
>
> **Members**
>
> Rahul --- Active
>
> Amit --- Active · Unclaimed
>
> Jhim --- Active
>
> **Archived**
>
> Rohan --- Archived

The member list is visible to approved group members.

Administrative actions are available only to users with appropriate
permissions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Role Badges

The UI uses icons plus text to communicate member roles and states.

Example:

> 👑 Sayantan `SUPER ADMIN`
>
> 🛡️ Priya `ADMIN`
>
> Rahul `MEMBER`
>
> Amit `UNCLAIMED`

Badges must not rely solely on color.

The exact iconography and visual treatment can be finalized during UI
design.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Promote Member to Admin

The Super Admin can promote an active member to Admin.

Example:

> Rahul
>
> **Make Admin**

Confirmation:

> **Make Rahul an Admin?**
>
> Admins can manage members, expenses, settlements, invitations and
> other administrative operations.
>
> **\[ Cancel \] \[ Make Admin \]**

After confirmation:

> Rahul `ADMIN`

The role change appears in Activity history.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Administrative Ownership Actions

Although Admins and Super Admin have the same day-to-day operational
permissions, ownership actions remain **Super Admin-only**.

Only the Super Admin can:

-   Promote members to Admin
-   Demote Admins
-   Transfer Super Admin ownership

Admins cannot perform these ownership/role-management actions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Demote Admin

The Super Admin can remove another Admin's Admin role.

Example:

> Priya --- Admin
>
> **Remove Admin Role**

Confirmation:

> **Remove Priya's Admin role?**

After confirmation:

> Priya --- Member

Priya immediately loses administrative permissions.

The change appears in Activity history.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Super Admin Cannot Demote Themselves

The Super Admin cannot directly remove their own Super Admin role.

They must first transfer Super Admin ownership.

This prevents a group from becoming ownerless.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Transfer Super Admin

The Super Admin can transfer ownership to another eligible active
member.

Example:

> **Transfer Super Admin**
>
> Priya --- Admin
>
> Rahul --- Admin
>
> Amit --- Member

Confirmation:

> **Transfer Super Admin to Priya?**
>
> You will become a regular Admin.
>
> **\[ Cancel \] \[ Transfer \]**

After transfer:

> Previous Super Admin → Admin
>
> New Super Admin → Super Admin

The transfer is recorded in Activity history.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Transfer to Unclaimed Member

Super Admin ownership cannot be transferred to an:

> **Active · Unclaimed**

member.

The new Super Admin must have a claimed/approved device associated with
the membership.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Super Admin Leaving

If the Super Admin attempts to leave:

> **You are the Super Admin.**
>
> Transfer Super Admin ownership before leaving this group.
>
> **\[ Transfer Ownership \]** **\[ Cancel \]**

After transferring ownership, the former Super Admin becomes an ordinary
Admin and may then leave normally.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Admin Leaving

An Admin can leave normally.

After leaving, the member is no longer active and historical
transactions remain intact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Normal Member Leaving

A normal member may leave voluntarily even if they have an outstanding
balance.

Example:

> Rahul owes Sayantan ₹600.

SettleZero warns:

> ⚠️ **You have an outstanding balance of ₹600.**
>
> Leaving will not remove this balance or your historical transactions.
>
> Your membership will become **Archived** so you won't be included in future expenses, and you can rejoin/reclaim it later.
>
> **\[ Cancel \] \[ Leave Anyway \]**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Leaving Does Not Delete Membership Identity

Leaving does not destroy the member identity.

The member becomes:

> **Archived**

This ensures they are excluded from participating in any new expenses, preventing accidental charges after departure.

Historical transactions remain attached to the member.

The member can later reclaim the identity by rejoining through an invite link.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Rejoining

A member who previously left can later use the invite flow and claim
their existing membership.

After Admin approval:

> **Active**

No duplicate member is created.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Rejoining With Outstanding Balance

If Rahul left while owing ₹600, the debt remains unchanged.

When Rahul rejoins:

> Rahul still owes ₹600.

Nothing is reset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. Admin Archiving Another Member

An Admin can archive an active member.

Example:

> Rahul
>
> **Archive Member**

Confirmation:

> **Archive Rahul?**
>
> Rahul will no longer participate in new expenses.
>
> Historical transactions will remain intact.
>
> **\[ Cancel \] \[ Archive Member \]**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. Archiving a Member With an Outstanding Balance

If Rahul owes ₹600, an Admin may still archive Rahul.

The balance remains exactly as it is.

SettleZero does not transfer, forgive, assign, or artificially settle the
debt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Archived Member Visibility

Archived members remain visible to all approved group members, separated
from active members.

Example:

> **Active Members**
>
> Rahul
>
> Priya
>
> **Archived Members**
>
> Amit
>
> Rohan

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. Archived Member in New Expenses

Archived members cannot participate in new expenses.

They cannot be selected as participant or payer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19. Archived Member as Payer

An archived member cannot be selected as payer for a new expense.

Historical expenses can still display:

> Paid by: Rohan

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. Archived Member and Existing Debt Settlement

An archived member can participate in a settlement when that settlement
reduces an existing debt.

Example:

> Rahul is archived.
>
> Rahul owes Sayantan ₹600.

SettleZero allows:

> Rahul → Sayantan ₹600

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. New Settlement for Archived Member

An archived member cannot be used to create an unrelated new settlement.

The rule is:

> **Archived members may participate in settlements only when the
> settlement reduces an existing outstanding debt.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Device Management

Because one member may have multiple devices, administrators can view
device associations.

Example:

> **Rahul**
>
> **Devices**
>
> 📱 Phone --- Active
>
> 💻 Tablet --- Active

Administrators can revoke device associations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. Revoke Device

An administrator can revoke a member's device.

Confirmation:

> **Revoke Rahul's device?**
>
> This device will no longer synchronize with the group.
>
> Rahul can rejoin/reclaim the membership from another device.
>
> **\[ Cancel \] \[ Revoke \]**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Lost Phone

If Rahul loses his phone:

1.  An Admin revokes the old device.
2.  Rahul installs SettleZero on the new phone.
3.  Rahul opens the group invite.
4.  Rahul selects **I'm already a member**.
5.  Rahul submits a claim for the existing Rahul identity.
6.  An Admin approves the claim.
7.  The new device becomes associated with Rahul.
8.  Existing data remains intact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 25. Self-Revoking a Device

A member can revoke their own current device.

This removes the current device association while keeping the member
active.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 26. No Devices

If all devices associated with a member are revoked:

> Rahul → **Active · Unclaimed**

The member identity and historical transactions remain intact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 27. Active · Unclaimed vs Archived

### Active · Unclaimed

The member is active but has no approved device.

They can:

-   Participate in expenses
-   Be payer
-   Owe money
-   Be owed money
-   Appear in balances
-   Participate in valid settlements

### Archived

The member is no longer active.

They cannot:

-   Participate in new expenses
-   Be payer for new expenses
-   Participate in normal active-member operations

They remain available for:

-   Historical transactions
-   Existing debt settlements
-   Balance history
-   Activity history
-   Future rejoining/reclaiming

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 28. Renaming Members

Members can be renamed.

A member can rename themselves.

Admins can rename members.

The new name must remain unique within the group.

The underlying member ID does not change.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 29. Name Change History

All meaningful member-name changes appear in Activity history.

If Rahul changes their own name:

> **Rahul changed their name to Rahul Roy**

If an Admin changes it:

> **Sayantan renamed Rahul → Rahul Roy**

The underlying member identity remains unchanged.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 30. Duplicate Names During Rename

A member cannot be renamed to an exact name already used by another
active member.

Example:

> **A member with this name already exists.**
>
> Please choose another name.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 31. Admin Renaming

Admins can rename any member.

This includes other Admins.

An Admin cannot change the Super Admin's ownership or role.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 32. Admin Renaming Another Admin

An Admin may rename another Admin.

However, an Admin cannot:

-   Demote another Admin
-   Promote another Admin
-   Transfer Super Admin ownership
-   Change Super Admin status

Those remain Super Admin-only operations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 33. Member Removal Terminology

The normal UI should not use:

> **Delete Member**

for ordinary membership management.

The preferred action is:

> **Archive Member**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 34. Administrative Activity History

Administrative membership changes appear in the group Activity feed.

Examples:

> Sayantan promoted Rahul to Admin.

> Priya archived Amit.

> Rahul reclaimed the existing Rahul membership.

> Sayantan transferred Super Admin to Priya.

> Priya revoked Rahul's old device.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 35. Administrative Notifications

Important administrative events can generate notifications.

Notifications should be sent only where they materially matter.

Examples:

> **You were promoted to Admin.**

> **Your membership claim was approved.**

> **Your membership claim was rejected.**

> **Your device was revoked.**

> **You were archived from Goa Trip.**

> **Your name was changed.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 36. Administrative Confirmations

Administrative/destructive actions require confirmation.

Examples include:

-   Archive member
-   Archive group
-   Transfer Super Admin
-   Revoke device
-   Demote Admin
-   Other destructive or ownership-sensitive operations

Permanent deletion, if implemented, should require substantially
stronger safeguards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 37. Group Archive

The Super Admin can archive the entire group.

Confirmation:

> **Archive Goa Trip 2026?**
>
> Existing members can still view the group's history.
>
> New members cannot join.
>
> New expenses and settlements cannot be created.
>
> **\[ Cancel \] \[ Archive Group \]**

An archived group is effectively:

> **Read-only for everyone**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 38. Restore Group

An archived group can be restored by the Super Admin.

After restoration:

> The group becomes active again.

Members retain their existing states.

Historical transactions remain unchanged.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 39. Invite Links After Restoration

When a group is archived, its invite link becomes unusable.

If the group is later restored:

> **A new invite link must be generated.**

The old invite link does not automatically become valid again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 40. Offline Expense After Group Archive

If the group is archived while a member's device is offline, the device
may not know immediately.

The member may create a local expense.

When synchronization occurs, the backend rejects the transaction because
the group was archived.

The local expense should not silently disappear.

SettleZero displays:

> ⚠️ **This group was archived before this expense could sync.**
>
> Your expense is still saved locally.
>
> **\[ Export \]**
>
> An Admin may need to restore the group before this expense can be
> synchronized.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 41. Offline Member Archival

If Rahul is archived while Rahul's device is offline, the device may not
know immediately.

Rahul may create a local expense.

When synchronization occurs, the server rejects the new transaction
because Rahul is archived.

The local transaction enters a synchronization error/conflict state
rather than being silently deleted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 42. Offline Administrative Conflicts

Example:

> Admin A offline → Archive Rahul
>
> Admin B offline → Promote Rahul to Admin

When synchronization occurs, the conflicting membership state
transitions require:

> **Manual Admin conflict resolution**

The application must not silently apply whichever device synchronized
last.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 43. Admin Authority

Admins have the same operational permissions as one another and as the
Super Admin, except for ownership/role-management actions.

An Admin may:

-   Edit/delete any expense
-   Restore deleted expenses
-   Edit/delete any settlement
-   Restore deleted settlements
-   Manage members operationally
-   Archive members
-   Rename members
-   Manage devices
-   Process membership requests
-   Manage invitations
-   Archive another Admin

However:

> **No Admin may archive, demote, or otherwise remove the Super Admin's
> ownership.**

# 48. Journey 5 Product Rules

### Roles

Super Admin and Admin have the same operational permissions.

### Ownership

Ownership and role-management actions remain Super Admin-only.

### Multiple Admins

A group may have multiple Admins.

### Super Admin continuity

A Super Admin must transfer ownership before leaving.

### Member continuity

Leaving or archiving does not delete a member identity.

### Active · Unclaimed

A member without an approved device remains usable in the group's
financial model.

### Archived members

Archived members cannot participate in new expenses.

### Existing debts

Archiving or leaving does not erase outstanding balances.

### Debt settlement

Archived members may settle existing outstanding debts.

### Device independence

Replacing a device does not replace the member identity.

### Device revocation

Admins can revoke devices; members can revoke their current device.

### Reclaiming

A member can reclaim an existing identity through the existing-member
claim process.

### Unique names

Exact duplicate active member names are not allowed within a group.

### Renaming

Renaming never creates a new member identity.

### Administrative transparency

Important administrative changes appear in Activity history.

### Administrative notifications

Only meaningful administrative events generate notifications.

### Confirmations

Destructive and ownership-sensitive actions require confirmation.

### Group archive

Archiving makes the group effectively read-only.

### Group restoration

Archived groups can be restored by the Super Admin.

### Invite regeneration

Restoring an archived group requires a new invite link.

### Offline state changes

Offline actions may temporarily succeed locally but are validated
against authoritative server state during synchronization.

### Conflict handling

Conflicting administrative state transitions require explicit
resolution.
